Research

Research verticals and centres

CRGD's research is organised into eighteen verticals. Each one asks a small number of hard questions about how Kerala and India are governed, and answers them with data, field work and comparison. The verticals talk to each other: few real policy problems stay inside one subject.

At a glance

20 research verticals

Choose a vertical to read its questions, see who works on it and find everything it has published.

  1. 01Governance, Finance and AccountabilityHow governments raise and spend public money, how they are held to account for it, and how the machinery of administration can serve citizens better.
  2. 02EducationFrom early childhood to university, how Kerala and India can move from near universal access to genuinely good learning for every student.
  3. 03Health and DemographyHow a society that has largely won the battles against early death and high fertility can meet its next challenges, from chronic disease to an ageing population.
  4. 04EmploymentWhy educated young people struggle to find work at home, why women's participation in paid work remains low, and how migration in and out of Kerala shapes the labour market.
  5. 05FederalismHow powers, money and responsibilities are shared between the Union, the states and local governments, and how that balance affects what Kerala can do for its people.
  6. 06Tourism and CultureHow tourism and cultural life can bring prosperity to communities without eroding the places, traditions and environments that attract visitors in the first place.
  7. 07MobilityHow people and goods move across a densely settled state, and how transport policy can make journeys safer, cleaner, fairer and quicker.
  8. 08Agriculture and Rural DevelopmentThe future of farming and rural livelihoods in a state where land is scarce, labour is costly and the climate is changing.
  9. 09Strategic Affairs and PlanningLong range planning for the state, and the strategic questions of security, diaspora and the Indian Ocean that matter to Kerala and to India.
  10. 10Science, Energy, Fintech and AIHow science, technology, clean energy and artificial intelligence can serve the public, and how they should be governed.
  11. 11Maritime EconomyPorts, shipping, fisheries and coastal communities, and how Kerala can build a maritime economy that is prosperous, fair and sustainable.
  12. 12Society, Law and PolicyHow laws are made, interpreted and enforced, and how they meet the realities of social life, from family and religion to speech and policing.
  13. 13Institutions and Social JusticeWhether public institutions treat every citizen fairly, and how policy can close the gaps that caste, gender, disability, tribe and poverty still produce.
  14. 14EconomyThe structure and prospects of Kerala's economy, and how growth can create good jobs, public revenue and broad prosperity.
  15. 15Environment and Disaster ManagementProtecting the ecology of the Western Ghats, the coast and the wetlands, and preparing communities for floods, landslides and a changing climate.
  16. 16Gender, State and PoliticsHow the state is governed in practice: the legislature, the executive, political parties and the political culture that shapes public decisions.
  17. 17UrbanisationHow a state where town and village blur into each other can plan for housing, water, waste, transport and public space.
  18. 18Elections, Narratives and PerceptionsHow citizens form political opinions, how elections are fought, and how narratives, media and misinformation shape public perceptions of policy.
  19. 19Foreign Policy and Global AffairsThe Foreign Policy and Global Affairs vertical at CRGD examines the forces shaping India’s engagement with the world and the wider transformation of the international order. Its work covers foreign policy, diplomacy, geopolitics, international institutions, regional and global security, strategic partnerships, economic statecraft, multilateralism, and emerging transnational challenges. The vertical seeks to produce rigorous, policy-relevant research that connects global developments with India’s strategic interests, democratic values, development priorities, and institutional choices. It also aims to create a platform for scholars, practitioners, and emerging researchers to engage with contemporary debates in international relations through research, commentary, dialogue, and public scholarship.
  20. 20Peace, Conflict and Security The Peace, Conflict and Security vertical at CRGD examines the causes, dynamics, and consequences of conflict, as well as the institutions and strategies that support peace, stability, and reconciliation. Its work spans conflict prevention, peacebuilding, mediation, security, humanitarian challenges, post-conflict reconstruction, identity and political violence, and the role of states, communities, and international institutions. The vertical aims to produce policy-relevant research that connects theoretical insight with practical approaches to preventing violence, strengthening resilience, and building more peaceful, inclusive, and accountable societies.
