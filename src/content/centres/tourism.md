shortTitle: Promoting sustainable, inclusive and responsible tourism through
  evidence-based research, policy advocacy and innovative approaches to tourism
  governance and development.
summary: >-
  The Tourism Vertical seeks to advance tourism as a catalyst for sustainable
  economic growth, cultural preservation, environmental conservation and
  inclusive regional development. It focuses on examining tourism policies,
  governance frameworks, institutional practices and emerging opportunities
  while addressing challenges relating to infrastructure, local livelihoods,
  ecological sustainability and community participation.


  Through interdisciplinary research, policy analysis, stakeholder engagement
  and constructive policy recommendations, the vertical aims to strengthen
  tourism governance and promote responsible, accessible and resilient tourism
  models. Particular emphasis will be placed on destination management, heritage
  and cultural tourism, ecotourism, community-based tourism, rural tourism,
  tourism entrepreneurship and the role of local governments in shaping tourism
  development.


  The vertical aspires to bridge research, policymaking, industry and
  communities, positioning tourism as a driver of livelihood generation,
  regional competitiveness, cultural exchange and sustainable development.
