Kerala's social development is impressive in averages, but averages can hide persistent gaps. Adivasi communities, Dalit households, fishing communities, persons with disabilities and many women experience a very different state from the one described in headline figures.

## Why it matters

The legitimacy of public institutions rests on fair treatment. Independent bodies such as human rights commissions, the Lok Ayukta, information commissions and commissions for specific communities exist to protect citizens. Welfare programs exist to support those with least. Whether they work as intended is a matter of evidence, and of justice.

## How we work

- **Gap analysis.** We use data to identify where outcomes differ between groups and regions.
- **Institutional review.** We study the work of oversight and rights bodies.
- **Listening studies.** We document the experience of people using welfare and public services.
- **Inclusion audits.** We help public bodies assess their accessibility and fairness.

The vertical works in partnership with community organisations and with the Society, Law and Policy vertical.
