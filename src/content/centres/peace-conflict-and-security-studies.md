title: "Peace, Conflict and Security "
summary: >-
  The Peace, Conflict and Security vertical at CRGD examines the causes,
  dynamics, and consequences of conflict, as well as the institutions and
  strategies that support peace, stability, and reconciliation. Its work spans
  conflict prevention, peacebuilding, mediation, security, humanitarian
  challenges, post-conflict reconstruction, identity and political violence, and
  the role of states, communities, and international institutions.


  The vertical aims to produce policy-relevant research that connects
  theoretical insight with practical approaches to preventing violence,
  strengthening resilience, and building more peaceful, inclusive, and
  accountable societies.
