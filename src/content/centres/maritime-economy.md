Kerala has a long coastline, a major port at Kochi, a new deep water transhipment port at Vizhinjam near Thiruvananthapuram, and fishing communities whose history goes back centuries. The maritime economy is one of the state's largest opportunities and most delicate balancing acts.

## Why it matters

Port led growth can bring investment, logistics and jobs. It can also change shorelines, displace livelihoods and strain infrastructure. Fishing communities face declining catches, coastal erosion, and exclusion from decisions that affect them. Getting the balance right requires evidence, consultation and long term thinking.

## How we work

- **Ports and logistics.** We study how ports are connected to the wider economy and what policies help local enterprise benefit.
- **Fishing communities.** We work with fishing communities to document livelihoods, risks and priorities.
- **Coastal change.** We review evidence on erosion, sea walls and nature based alternatives.
- **Blue economy.** We examine marine tourism, aquaculture and shipbuilding.

The vertical works with the Strategic Affairs and Planning and Environment verticals. Being based in Thiruvananthapuram, CRGD is close to the coast it studies.
