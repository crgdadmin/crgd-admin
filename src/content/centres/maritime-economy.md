shortTitle: Advancing sustainable energy systems and a resilient maritime

summary: >-
  The Energy and Maritime Affairs Vertical of the Centre for Research on
  Governance and Development (CRGD) seeks to strengthen policy research,
  institutional governance and strategic thinking in the interconnected domains
  of energy and maritime development. It examines the role of energy security,
  sustainable energy transitions, maritime infrastructure, ocean governance and
  the blue economy in shaping economic growth, national security and regional
  cooperation.


  The vertical focuses on key policy areas, including conventional and renewable
  energy, energy access and affordability, energy transition, critical
  resources, port-led development, shipping and logistics, maritime trade,
  coastal economies, fisheries, marine resource management and regional maritime
  connectivity. It also explores emerging opportunities and challenges arising
  from geopolitical developments, technological advancements, climate change and
  evolving global energy and trade dynamics.


  Through interdisciplinary research, policy briefs, stakeholder consultations
  and engagement with government institutions, industry, academia and civil
  society, the vertical aims to generate actionable policy recommendations and
  promote transparent, efficient and sustainable governance frameworks.


  With particular attention to India's strategic interests and the wider Indian
  Ocean Region, the vertical aspires to contribute to energy resilience,
  maritime cooperation, economic competitiveness and sustainable development.
