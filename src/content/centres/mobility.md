Kerala's settlements run almost continuously along its coast and midlands, which makes transport both essential and difficult. Roads are crowded, private vehicle ownership has grown quickly and road safety remains a serious concern.

## Why it matters

Transport decisions shape where people can live and work, how much time they lose to travel, what they breathe and whether they arrive safely. Large projects such as metro lines, highways and proposed rail corridors involve heavy public investment and often provoke strong disagreement. Smaller decisions, such as bus routes, footpaths and junction design, affect more people every day.

## How we work

- **Project appraisal.** We review the economic, social and environmental case for major transport investments, using published data.
- **Public transport.** We study the finances and services of public bus operators and metro systems.
- **Road safety.** We analyse crash data and the effectiveness of enforcement, engineering and education.
- **Active travel.** We document how streets work for pedestrians, cyclists, children and older people.

The vertical works with the Urbanisation and Environment verticals and with transport user groups.
