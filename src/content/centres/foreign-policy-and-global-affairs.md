title: Foreign Policy and Global Affairs
summary: >-
  The Foreign Policy and Global Affairs vertical at CRGD examines the forces
  shaping India’s engagement with the world and the wider transformation of the
  international order. Its work covers foreign policy, diplomacy, geopolitics,
  international institutions, regional and global security, strategic
  partnerships, economic statecraft, multilateralism, and emerging transnational
  challenges.


  The vertical seeks to produce rigorous, policy-relevant research that connects
  global developments with India’s strategic interests, democratic values,
  development priorities, and institutional choices. It also aims to create a
  platform for scholars, practitioners, and emerging researchers to engage with
  contemporary debates in international relations through research, commentary,
  dialogue, and public scholarship.
