title: Demography and Migration
shortTitle: Advancing evidence-based research and policy solutions on
  demographic change, population dynamics and human mobility to promote
  inclusive development and effective governance.
summary: >-
  The Demography and Migration Vertical of the Centre for Research on Governance
  and Development (CRGD) seeks to examine the changing dynamics of populations
  and human mobility and their implications for governance, economic
  development, social transformation and public policy. It focuses on
  understanding demographic transitions, population growth and decline, ageing,
  fertility, mortality, urbanisation, internal and international migration,
  displacement and changing patterns of settlement.


  The vertical explores the economic, social, political and institutional
  dimensions of migration, including labour mobility, remittances, migrant
  rights and welfare, diaspora engagement, migration governance, refugee
  protection and the challenges of irregular migration. It also examines the
  implications of demographic change for employment, education, healthcare,
  social security, housing, urban planning and regional development.


  Through interdisciplinary research, demographic analysis, policy evaluations,
  stakeholder consultations and evidence-based recommendations, the vertical
  aims to contribute to informed policymaking and responsive institutional
  frameworks. Particular attention will be given to emerging demographic trends
  in India and South Asia, including Kerala's distinctive demographic
  transition, international migration patterns, ageing population and changing
  labour-market dynamics.


  The vertical aspires to bridge demographic research and public policy,
  promoting migration systems that are humane, equitable and
  development-oriented while strengthening the capacity of governments and
  institutions to respond effectively to changing population realities.
