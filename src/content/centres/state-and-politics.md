Kerala's politics is intensely participatory, closely contested and often polarised. This vertical studies the institutions and practices of government and politics with the same evidence based approach CRGD applies to policy.

## Why it matters

Policy outcomes depend on how governments are formed, how legislatures function and how parties compete. Strong scrutiny of the executive improves decisions. Weak scrutiny allows mistakes to go unchallenged. Understanding the political system is essential for anyone who wants to change policy.

## How we work

- **Legislative monitoring.** We track sittings, debates, questions and committee work in the state assembly.
- **Comparative analysis.** We compare institutions and practices across Indian states.
- **Political history.** We document how key policies were made, debated and changed.
- **Civic education.** We produce plain language guides to how the state government works.

CRGD is non partisan. This vertical does not support or oppose any party, and its researchers follow CRGD's policy on political neutrality.
