**Thiruvananthapuram, 10 July 2026.** A special report published today by CRGD calls for landslide susceptibility maps at the level of local government wards and for evacuation plans tied to clear rainfall warning triggers.

The report, [After the Landslides: Building Safer Hill Communities](/publications/special-reports/after-the-landslides), brings together research on geology, land use, early warning and rehabilitation.
