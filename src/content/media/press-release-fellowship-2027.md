**Thiruvananthapuram, 5 October 2026.** CRGD today opened applications for its [Public Policy Fellowship 2027](/programs/fellowships/public-policy-fellowship-2027), a nine month, full time program for early career researchers.

Fellows will join one of CRGD's research verticals and design, carry out and publish a policy research project under the supervision of senior researchers.

The closing date for applications is 30 November 2026.
