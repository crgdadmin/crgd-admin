shortBio: >-
  Jayashri Ramesh Sundaram is a Ph.D. student in Communication at the University
  of Maryland. She has nearly a decade of professional experience working with
  non-governmental organisations, corporations and government stakeholders
  across India's social sector. Her professional experience spans monitoring and
  evaluation, impact communications, fundraising and programme management,
  complemented by extensive field research across initiatives in gender, health,
  education and rural development.


  Drawing on her professional and field experience, her academic interests focus
  on strategic communication practices, organisational discourse, and the
  communication channels and tools through which corporations and
  non-governmental organisations establish and manage cross-sector partnerships.
  She is particularly interested in how organisations communicate and negotiate
  shared goals, roles, accountability and expectations, and how these
  communication processes influence collaboration and social impact.
