  Abhijay A. is a policy analyst, academic, columnist, and independent
  researcher with a keen interest in public policy, international relations,
  global governance, and political economy. He holds a master's degree in
  Political Science, specialising in International Relations.


  His research and writing engage with contemporary political developments,
  democratic governance, human rights, climate policy, international affairs,
  and institutional accountability. Through independent research, academic
  engagement, and contributions to public discourse, he critically examines
  complex policy questions and connects scholarly perspectives to the practical
  realities of governance and society.


  With an academic background in Political Science and experience in teaching,
  research, and policy analysis, he brings an interdisciplinary perspective to
  contemporary political and policy debates. A commitment to fostering critical
  inquiry drives his work, informed public discourse, and a deeper understanding
  of national and international developments.


  At the Centre for Research on Governance and Development (CRGD), he serves as
  Executive Director – Academy and Policy Strategy and a Member of the Governing
  Body. In this capacity, he contributes to institutional strategy, policy
  development, academic engagement, and international collaborations. His
  responsibilities include strengthening CRGD's engagement with universities,
  research institutions, and policy stakeholders; developing meaningful
  institutional partnerships; and advancing evidence-informed research,
  dialogue, and policy initiatives on issues of national and international
  significance.
