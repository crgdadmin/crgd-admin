shortBio: >-
  Prof. Josukutty C. A. is a Professor of Political Science at the University of
  Kerala, currently serving as Campus Director of the Kerala University Campus
  and Honorary Director of the Survey Research Centre. He holds an M.A. in
  Political Science (First Rank), an M.Phil., and a Ph.D. from the University of
  Kerala, and has over two decades of postgraduate teaching experience. His
  areas of specialisation include International Relations, Indian Foreign
  Policy, Indian Politics, and Human Rights.


  An accomplished academic and researcher, he has guided 13 Ph.D. scholars,
  authored and edited 17 books, published over 60 peer-reviewed and
  Scopus-indexed journal articles, and contributed numerous book chapters and
  nearly 200 popular articles in English and Malayalam. He has undertaken
  research projects funded by the UGC and other institutions and presented over
  100 papers at national and international conferences.


  He previously served as Director of the School of Social Sciences (2022–2025),
  Director of the UGC-Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Centre (2024–2026), and
  Head of the Department of Political Science. A Fulbright-Nehru Fellow (2015),
  he has served as Visiting Faculty at the University of South Dakota and
  participated in several international academic programmes. A recipient of the
  University of Kerala's Academic Excellence Award (2019), he has made
  significant contributions to academic administration, faculty development,
  public scholarship, and community engagement.
