shortBio: Tushita Bhandari is a researcher, public policy analyst, and doctoral
  scholar at the Nelson Mandela Centre for Peace and Conflict Resolution, Jamia
  Millia Islamia, New Delhi. Her research focuses on India–China relations,
  strategic affairs, and the evolving dynamics of asymmetric warfare in the
  digital age. Her broader research interests encompass international relations,
  geopolitics, border security, emerging technologies, cybersecurity, digital
  governance, and India's foreign and security policy. She has previously worked
  with the Observer Research Foundation (ORF), where she contributed to research
  programmes and international policy initiatives. Her work explores the
  intersections of geopolitics, technology, national security, and public
  policy.
