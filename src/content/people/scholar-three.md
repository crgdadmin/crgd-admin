shortBio: >-
  Dr. Aditya Agrawal is a legal academic and practitioner and currently serves
  as an Assistant Professor at O.P. Jindal Global University, Sonipat, India.


  He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Law from KIIT University, an LL.M. from
  National Law University, Delhi, completed in 2020, and a Ph.D. from
  Hidayatullah National Law University, Raipur. He also served as an editor for
  the WTO–WIPO Colloquium Papers in 2020.


  Dr. Agrawal’s professional experience spans both academia and legal practice.
  From December 2020 to July 2022, he practised before the High Court of
  Chhattisgarh at Bilaspur, where he argued in more than 100 cases. Since August
  2022, he has been associated with O.P. Jindal Global University, contributing
  to legal education and scholarship.
