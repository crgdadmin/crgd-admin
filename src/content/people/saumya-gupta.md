shortBio: >-
  Saumya Gupta is currently a PhD candidate at the University of Amsterdam,
  where her research examines the Indian Census and its relationship with
  emerging and new religious identities and movements, including Lingayat,
  Sarna, Ravidassia, Ayya Vazhi, and Ananda Marga. She was previously a Fellow
  at FLAME University, where she taught a course on Indian Politics and the
  Constitution. She completed her MA at the University of Chicago, following her
  BTech in Computer Science from DA-IICT, India.


  She has led the Political Intelligence Units for Punjab and Gujarat and served
  as one of the state leads in the Field and Political Liaison Unit as a
  Principal Associate at the Indian Political Action Committee (I-PAC). During
  her tenure, the organisation's teams were associated with successful election
  campaigns in West Bengal and Tamil Nadu. She has also worked in Data Science
  and Analytics at Publicis Media in London, Capital One in Virginia, USA, and
  Deloitte in Hyderabad, India.


  Saumya has also produced programmes for Media Vigil, an independent Hindi
  media platform, focusing on issues concerning caste and tribal communities. In
  this capacity, she has interviewed prominent personalities, including
  Naseeruddin Shah, Ravish Kumar, Yogendra Yadav, Pavan K. Varma and Manoj Jha.
  Her writings have appeared in several publications, including The Wire,
  NewsClick, The Indian Express, Outlook, The Hindu, The News Minute, The Scroll
  and Media Vigil, in both English and Hindi.
