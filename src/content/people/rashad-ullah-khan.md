shortBio: Rashad Ullah Khan is a researcher and writer whose work explores the
  intersections of religion, identity, politics and public policy in
  contemporary India. He is pursuing an M.A. in Religion at Columbia University
  and holds undergraduate and postgraduate degrees from the Tata Institute of
  Social Sciences (TISS). With over six years of research experience, he has
  worked with institutions including the Centre for Development Policy and
  Practice and the Kautilya School of Public Policy. His writings have appeared
  in The Diplomat, Berkeley Journal of Sociology and Siasat, among other
  publications. At Open Tab, he serves as Section Editor for Religion, Identity
  & Politics, bringing research-driven perspectives to discussions on society
  and public life.
