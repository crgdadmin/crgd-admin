shortBio: Dr. S. Assa Aravindh is a Project Manager and Docent (Adjunct
  Professor) in the Sustainable Chemistry Research Units, Faculty of Technology,
  at the University of Oulu, Finland. A physicist specialising in condensed
  matter theory, she focuses her research on computational materials science,
  advanced energy materials, and sustainable technologies. She holds a Ph.D. in
  Physics from the University of Madras, with doctoral research conducted at the
  Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR) under a Department of Atomic
  Energy scholarship. She has held postdoctoral positions at the Institute of
  Mathematical Sciences, Chennai, and King Abdullah University of Science and
  Technology (KAUST), along with visiting research positions at JNCASR,
  Bengaluru, and the Tyndall National Institute, Ireland. She has also held a
  Career Development Fellowship at Durham University, UK. She currently leads
  the M-ERA.NET GENSIB project on sodium-ion battery cathodes and contributes to
  research exploring quantum computing applications in battery technologies.
