shortBio: >-
  Dr. Manpreet Kaur Gill holds a Ph.D. in Economics from SOAS, University of
  London; a Postgraduate Diploma in Economic Research from the University of
  Manchester; an M.Phil. in Economics from Jawaharlal Nehru University; and an
  M.Sc. in Economics from Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar.


  With over eight years of international experience in economic research and
  policy analysis, she has developed expertise in institutional research, data
  management, statistical analysis, and academic planning. She is proficient in
  research and analytical tools, including SPSS, STATA, NVivo, and R. Her
  research interests encompass globalisation, migration, labour economics, and
  higher education systems, informing her understanding of institutional
  development, policy evaluation, and academic quality assurance.


  Her teaching experience includes courses in Microeconomics and Macroeconomics,
  with responsibilities spanning curriculum delivery, student assessment, and
  academic development. She has also contributed to academic operations,
  curriculum planning, and institutional research, supporting quality education
  and effective academic administration in multicultural and international
  higher education environments.
