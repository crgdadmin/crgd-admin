shortBio: >-
  Rejimon Kuttappan is an investigative journalist and author with nearly two
  decades of experience reporting on forced labour and migration. He has
  documented labour exploitation across India’s shrimp, steel, textile, and
  sandstone industries, and his reporting contributed to Indian shrimp being
  included on the U.S. Department of Labor’s list of goods associated with
  forced labour.


  He has reported for the Associated Press on scam centres in Southeast Asia and
  writes for Al Jazeera and The Hindu. His work has also involved collaborations
  with Human Rights Watch on issues concerning FIFA World Cup workers and
  seafarers stranded in the Strait of Hormuz, as well as engagements with the
  ILO, Kelley Drye & Warren, Corporate Accountability Lab, and Issara Institute.


  He is the author of five books, including Undocumented (Penguin) and River of
  Grey Flowers (Speaking Tiger). A Reuters and ILO Fellow, he has spoken at the
  UN Responsible Business and Human Rights Forum, Asia-Pacific, on fair work in
  2025 and India’s Labour Codes in 2026.
