shortBio: >-
  Sagarika Ghose is a Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha) representing the All
  India Trinamool Congress. She is an eminent journalist, author and public
  commentator with an extensive career spanning print and broadcast journalism.


  She is the author of two acclaimed political biographies: Indira: India's Most
  Powerful Prime Minister (Juggernaut, 2017) and Atal Bihari Vajpayee: India's
  Most Loved Prime Minister (Juggernaut, 2021), both of which have been declared
  national bestsellers. She also authored the critically acclaimed work Why I Am
  a Liberal: A Manifesto for Indians Who Believe in Individual Freedom (Penguin,
  2018). Her literary contributions include two novels, The Gin Drinkers
  (HarperCollins, 1999) and Blind Faith (HarperCollins, 2005).


  Ghose has worked as a journalist since 1991, serving at prominent publications
  including The Times of India, Outlook and The Indian Express. She was a
  prime-time news anchor on BBC World's Question Time India and subsequently
  worked with CNN-IBN from 2005 to 2014, where she hosted programmes including
  Face the Nation and India 360, and served as Deputy Editor.


  She received her undergraduate education at Loreto Convent and Delhi Public
  School, R.K. Puram. She graduated with First Class Honours in History from St.
  Stephen's College, University of Delhi (1983–1986). A recipient of the
  prestigious Rhodes Scholarship, she pursued postgraduate studies at the
  University of Oxford, United Kingdom, earning an MA and M.Phil. in History and
  International Relations.


  Ghose has received several prestigious awards and recognitions for her
  contributions to journalism and public discourse. Her audience-interaction
  programme Question Time Didi, featuring West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata
  Banerjee, received the NT Award for Best Public Debate Show in 2013. She
  received the Indian Television Academy (ITA) Award for Excellence in
  Journalism in 2009 and the ITA Best Anchor Award in 2013. In 2012, St.
  Stephen's College honoured her with the C.F. Andrews Award for Distinguished
  Alumnus.


  In 2014, The Rhodes Project featured her among 13 prominent women Rhodes
  Scholars. In 2017, she received the C.H. Mohammed Koya National Award for
  Journalism.


  In 2024, Ghose was elected to the Rajya Sabha.
