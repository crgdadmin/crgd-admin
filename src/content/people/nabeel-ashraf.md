shortBio: Nabeel Asharaf is an economist whose research focuses on India's
  development, energy, and environmental policy. He holds an MSc in Development
  Economics from the University of Sussex. He currently serves as a Research
  Assistant at the Kerala State Planning Board and previously worked as a
  Research Assistant to Dr. C. Rangarajan at MSE, contributing to research on
  economic growth, inflation, and corporate investment. His joint research with
  renowned environmental economist Richard Tol on the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala
  Yojana was published in Economic Analysis and Policy. He has experience
  working with household- and firm-level survey datasets, including IHDS, DHS
  and ASI, using Stata and R. His current research interests include the health
  and economic effects of air pollution in India.
