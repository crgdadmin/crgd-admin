shortBio: >-
  Dr. Nanda Kishor M. S. is Head and Associate Professor in the Department of
  Politics and International Studies at Pondicherry University. He also holds
  the Sri Aurobindo Chair, serves as a member of the NCERT Textbook Committee
  under the Ministry of Education, and coordinates the SAP-DRS II programme at
  Pondicherry University. He previously served as Head of the Department of
  Geopolitics and International Relations at the Manipal Academy of Higher
  Education (MAHE), Manipal.


  He holds an M.Phil. and Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of
  Hyderabad (formerly Hyderabad Central University), India, and completed his
  postdoctoral research at Leiden University, the Netherlands, with an Erasmus
  Mundus Fellowship from the European Union. His teaching and research interests
  encompass the Geopolitics of West Asia, India's Foreign Policy, the Concepts
  of War and Peace in Geopolitics, Terrorism, and Asymmetric Conflicts.


  Dr. Kishor has received several prestigious research fellowships and grants,
  including short-term research fellowships from the United Nations High
  Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), the Brookings Institution, the Government
  of Finland, and the Maulana Abul Kalam Azad Institute of Asian Studies (MCRG),
  Kolkata. In 2012, he visited Hochschule Bremen – City University of Applied
  Sciences, Germany, under a German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)-sponsored
  programme. He was also selected for the International Visitor Leadership
  Program (IVLP) of the United States Department of State in June–July 2013.


  He serves on the Advisory Boards of REVA University, Bengaluru, and the INDIC
  Research Forum. He is a Senior Fellow at Defence Research and Studies (DRaS),
  India; a Non-Resident Fellow at the Middle East Institute, New Delhi; an
  Advisor and Subject Expert to COVINTS, a Bengaluru-based risk analysis
  company; an Analyst with Islamic Theology of Counter-Terrorism, London; and a
  Visiting Fellow at AIDIA, Kathmandu. He has also served as Features Editor
  (Security Studies) at the Science, Technology and Security Forum, MAHE (MARG).


  An accomplished academic and author, Dr. Kishor has published extensively in
  scholarly journals and edited volumes. His recent book, Reimagining India in
  the Geopolitics of the 21st Century, has received critical appreciation for
  its thematic relevance and intellectual contribution. He is currently working
  on four forthcoming books: India and the Great Powers in the Indo-Pacific:
  Praxis of a Geopolitical Imagination; Strategy and Negotiations in
  International Relations; Understanding Perception of South Asian States on
  Rising India: Integrating Panchamrit with India's Neighbourhood First Policy;
  and The Art of Governing: Revisiting Kautilya's Arthashastra for the 21st
  Century.


  Dr. Kishor is currently heading a Major Research Project sponsored by the
  Indian Council of Social Science Research (ICSSR). He has also served as
  visiting faculty at several Human Resource Development Centres (HRDCs) and
  prestigious training institutions, including the Indian Naval Academy,
  Ezhimala, Kerala; the College of Air Warfare, Secunderabad; and the Karnataka
  Police Academy, Mysuru.


  Beyond his academic and research engagements, Dr. Kishor is a sought-after
  public speaker on Dharma, Arthashastra, and the Indian Knowledge System,
  alongside his areas of specialisation in Geopolitics and International
  Relations. A keen enthusiast of languages, he is fluent in seven languages.
