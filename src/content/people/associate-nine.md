  Dr. Sruthi S. K. is a materials science researcher and sustainability expert

  infrastructure strengthening, retrofitting, and resilient construction.

  intersection of materials innovation, infrastructure sustainability, and
  environmental impact assessment.


  Her broader research interests encompass sustainable infrastructure,
  climate-resilient development, the circular economy, decarbonisation, and
  evidence-based environmental policymaking. She is particularly interested in
  bridging the gap between scientific research, industrial practices, and public
  policy to advance sustainable development.
