shortBio: >-
  Dr. Amna Mirza is an academician, author, social entrepreneur, and artist,
  currently serving as an Associate Professor at SPM College, University of
  Delhi. She is an alumna of St. Stephen’s College and Hindu College, University
  of Delhi, and holds M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees from the Department of Political
  Science, University of Delhi.


  Her research spans digital transformation, Industry 4.0, international
  political economy, globalization, foreign affairs, digital society, and
  international relations. She has authored ten books, published over twenty
  research papers, and participated in more than 200 national and international
  conferences as an invited speaker, paper presenter, and resource person.


  Dr. Mirza has supervised doctoral research, undertaken funded projects,
  contributed to national MOOCs and online learning initiatives, and holds
  patents granted in India. She has also completed advanced programmes,
  including courses from HarvardX, and has had academic engagements with
  institutions such as the University of Duisburg-Essen, Germany, and the
  University of Fribourg, Switzerland.


  Her recognitions include the Godfrey Phillips Golden Ovary Award, St.
  Stephen’s College Centenary Medal for Character Combined with Learning, Bharat
  Nirman Award for Women Entrepreneurship, Mook-Nayak National Award, and
  Business Rankers Women Achievement Award. Her co-authored book, Digital
  Transformation, Artificial Intelligence & Society (Springer), recently
  received a Top Cited Book Award in Business, Economics, Politics & Law for
  2021–2025 at the Springer Nature Books Summit.


  She is also Project Director of an ICSSR-funded research project on “Women, AI
  & Agriculture” under the Punyashloka Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Special Call for
  Women-Led Research on Women-Led Development, Government of India.


  Beyond academia, Dr. Mirza contributes opinion pieces and columns to leading
  newspapers in multiple languages and is actively involved in cultural,
  literary, and artistic forums. Her work reflects a deeply interdisciplinary
  and globally engaged approach to scholarship, public life, and social
  innovation.
