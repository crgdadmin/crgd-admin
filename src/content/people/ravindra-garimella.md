shortBio: >-
  Ravindra Garimella is a distinguished parliamentary expert and former senior
  officer of the Lok Sabha Secretariat, Parliament of India, with over 30 years
  of experience in parliamentary affairs, legislative procedures and
  institutional governance. He served as Joint Secretary (Legislation), heading
  the Legislative Division until 2019, and spent 25 years as Officer at the
  Table of the Lok Sabha. His extensive experience encompasses legislative
  business, parliamentary privileges, anti-defection matters, budgetary
  processes and parliamentary committees.


  He has participated in international parliamentary study programmes in
  Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States, and has served as
  Visiting Fellow at the Universities of Warwick and Nottingham. He has
  delivered guest lectures at King's College London and the London School of
  Economics. A regular contributor to The Parliamentarian since 2005, he has
  authored and contributed to several publications on parliamentary practices
  and procedures, including An Introduction to Indian Parliament: Process and
  Practices (Routledge, UK). He has also served as a faculty member at the
  Parliamentary Research and Training Institute for Democracies (PRIDE) and
  other institutions.


  He has served as Private Secretary to the Union Minister for Tribal Affairs
  and Panchayati Raj and as Assistant Returning Officer for the 2017
  Presidential Election. He is currently Secretary to the Leader of the
  Opposition in the Rajya Sabha and Senior Advisor to 52 Gates, Knowledge Centre
  – Legal.
