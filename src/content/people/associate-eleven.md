shortBio: Siddhartha Patil is a lawyer and legal policy researcher working at

  legal and policy reforms, particularly on shaping laws and institutions to
  promote good governance, transparency, and effective public administration. He
  holds a B.A. LL.B. from the University of Pune and an M.Sc. in Public Policy
  and Administration from the London School of Economics and Political Science
  (LSE). Before transitioning into policy research, he practised at the Bombay
  High Court, Nagpur Bench, and worked in political consulting. His broader
  interests include law, politics, and public policy, with a particular focus on
  how legal frameworks and political processes shape societies and institutions.
