shortBio: >-
  Dr. Areeba Ahsanat Moazzam is an Assistant Professor at the School of Liberal
  Arts and Humanities, Woxsen University, Hyderabad. She holds a Ph.D., M.Phil.,
  and M.A. in Political Science from the University of Hyderabad.


  In 2025, she represented India as one of only 16 scholars selected globally
  for the U.S. Department of State’s Study of the U.S. Institutes (SUSI) for
  Scholars programme on U.S. Foreign Policy, held over six weeks at the
  University of Delaware, New York, and Washington, D.C.


  She is also completing an international research project supported by the
  AASSREC International Collaboration and Development Grant titled “The Impact
  of Artificial Intelligence on Employment and Workforce Dynamics in Hyderabad,
  India: Challenges, Strategies, and Policy Recommendations.”


  Her areas of specialisation include American Studies, Indo-Pacific Studies,
  Political Science, and International Relations. She has presented her research
  at international conferences in Dubai and Delhi and has published in refereed
  journals and edited volumes with publishers including Springer and World
  Scientific.


  Dr. Moazzam has also served as a subject matter expert for the UGC and Maulana
  Azad National Urdu University, contributing to M.A. distance and online
  programmes.


  At Woxsen University, she currently serves as Co-Chair of Indo-Pacific
  Studies, where she contributes to research, organises webinars and academic
  events, and serves as Editor-in-Chief of the annual magazine.
