shortBio: >-
  Ammu S. Anil is a Visiting Fellow at NIICE Nepal, Lalitpur, and a Senior
  Research Fellow at the MMAJ Academy of International Studies, Jamia Millia
  Islamia, New Delhi. She is currently pursuing her doctoral research on
  “Hydro-diplomacy in the Himalayas: A Case Study of India–Nepal Relations”
  under the supervision of Prof. Mathew Joseph C.


  Her academic interests include international relations theory, constitutional
  studies, sustainable development, environmental politics, climate education,
  gender, public policy, and feminist foreign policy.


  She has completed an internship with NIICE and the Feminist Foreign Policy
  Fellowship with IMPRI and The Asia Foundation. Her publications include
  research papers, book reviews, commentaries, and book chapters, with
  contributions appearing in platforms and publications associated with
  Routledge, SAGE, South Asian Voices, and the Lowy Institute.
