shortBio: >-
  Jobial Alex is a historian of South Asia whose research lies at the
  intersection of environmental history and science and technology studies. He
  is currently pursuing his doctoral research at Binghamton University, State
  University of New York (SUNY). He holds a Master's degree in History from
  Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU) and a Bachelor's degree in History from St.
  Stephen's College, Delhi. Prior to joining Binghamton University, he was a UGC
  Junior Research Fellow at IIT Guwahati.


  He has presented his research at several international academic conferences,
  including the North American Conference on British Studies, the American
  Society for Environmental History, and the Annual Conference on South Asia in
  Madison. Over the past six years, he has developed and taught several courses
  in Environmental History and Animal Studies. He has also held various
  leadership positions at Binghamton University, particularly in the Faculty
  Senate and the Graduate Council.
