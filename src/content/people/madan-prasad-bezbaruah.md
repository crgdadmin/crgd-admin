shortBio: >-
  Mr. Madan Prasad Bezbaruah is a former Secretary to the Government of India,
  Ministry of Tourism, and the longest-serving incumbent of the position. He
  also served as Member of the North Eastern Council in the rank of Minister of
  State. His distinguished administrative career includes senior assignments
  across the Government of Assam, the Planning Commission, the Reserve Bank of
  India and the Indian High Commission in London. He chaired the committee
  constituted by the Ministry of Home Affairs to examine the concerns of people
  from the North East, popularly known as the Bezbaruah Committee.


  An internationally recognised tourism expert, he has served as Chairman of the
  Pacific Asia Travel Association (PATA), Strategic Group member and Honorary
  Permanent Representative of UN Tourism, and consultant to the UN, World Bank
  and Asian Development Bank. He has also chaired key RBI committees and served
  on NITI Aayog's NITI Forum for the North East. An accomplished author, he has
  written four books on tourism and served as Chief Editor of the five-volume
  encyclopaedia *Fairs and Festivals of India*. He holds a Master's degree in
  Economics from the Delhi School of Economics and a Master of Public
  Administration from Harvard University.
