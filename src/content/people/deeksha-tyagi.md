**Deeksha Tyagi** is an interdisciplinary researcher, political consultant and writer, a Don Lavoie Fellow at the Mercatus Center at George Mason University, USA and a postgraduate student of History. Her work examines how ideas, institutions, and historical narratives shape political and social life in India.

She has worked with the Ideas of India project under Professor Rahul Sagar (New York University), and Professor S. N. Balagangadhara (University of Ghent). She was also a Research Associate at the **Sri Aurobindo Centre for Advanced Research, Pondicherry**, where she authored a monograph reading Rabindranath Tagore’s *Gitanjali* through Sri Aurobindo’s poetics, currently under publication. Her research spans Indian political thought and Puranic literature, alongside questions of community authority, gender, electoral behaviour, and heritage conservation. She has undertaken archaeological fieldwork with the Archaeological Survey of India and conducted policy research, including constituency-level work across western Uttar Pradesh. Her experience includes legislative research, electoral analysis, and policy briefs.

Her write-ups have appeared in *The Hindu*, *The Wire*, and *ThePrint*. She was elected to the Royal Historical Society in 2026. Alongside institutional research, Deeksha is committed to recovering histories absent from formal archives through oral traditions, regional literature, and Rajasthan’s cultural landscapes. A poet as well as a researcher, she sees scholarship as a public undertaking, one that should shape how societies interpret their past and deliberate about their future. She welcomes collaborations in historical research, public policy, political thought, and cultural heritage.
