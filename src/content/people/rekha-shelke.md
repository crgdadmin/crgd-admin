shortBio: Professor (Dr.) Rekha Shelke is the Dean of the Faculty of Social
  Sciences and Humanities at MGM University, Aurangabad. An academician, media
  educator, and researcher with over 35 years of combined industry and academic
  experience, she previously served as Assistant Editor, Advertisement Manager,
  and News Editor at Daily Lokpatra. Her academic and institutional roles
  include serving on the Board of Management at MGM University, chairing MGM
  Publication House, and serving as Director of BaiManus Media Research
  Foundation and MGM Community Radio 90.8 FM. An approved Ph.D. guide, she has
  authored and edited several books and published over 25 research papers on
  development communication, media ethics, artificial intelligence, and women's
  empowerment. Her work focuses on advancing ethical, socially responsive, and
  professionally oriented media education.
