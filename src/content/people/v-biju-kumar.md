shortBio: "Prof. V. Bijukumar is a Professor and former Chairperson of the
  Centre for Political Studies, School of Social Sciences, Jawaharlal Nehru
  University (JNU), New Delhi. He previously taught Political Science at Mizoram
  University, Aizawl, and North-Eastern Hill University, Shillong. In 2020, he
  served as India's part-time country researcher for the Rethinking Parties in
  Democratizing Asia project under the Regional Programme Political Dialogue
  Asia of the Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung, Singapore. His research interests
  include Comparative Politics, the Political Economy of Development, and
  Society and Politics in Northeast India. He has published over 100 research
  articles in peer-reviewed national and international journals and edited
  volumes, and has contributed to regional and national newspapers and
  magazines. His books include Deliberative Democracy and its Encounters: India
  and the World (2026), Media and its Transformation in India: Syntax of
  Corporatisation and Exclusivity (co-authored, 2025), New Trends in Emerging
  Power–Great Power Conflict (co-authored, 2024), Parties and Electoral Politics
  in Northeast India: Contention of Ethno-regionalism and Hindu Nationalism
  (2019), and Reinventing the Congress: Economic Policies and Strategies since
  1991 (2006)."
