The colloquium is where CRGD's research is tested before it is published. A researcher presents work in progress for thirty minutes; discussants and the audience respond for an hour.

Papers are circulated a week in advance to registered participants. Scholars, students and practitioners are welcome. To join the mailing list or to propose a paper, write to us.
