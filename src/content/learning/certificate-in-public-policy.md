The Foundation Certificate in Public Policy is designed for graduates, young professionals, civil society workers, journalists and anyone who wants a structured introduction to public policy in India.

## Modules

1. **How policy is made.** The Constitution, the Union, states and local governments; legislatures, executives and courts.
2. **Public money.** Budgets, taxes, borrowing and transfers.
3. **Evidence.** Finding, reading and judging data and research.
4. **Policy analysis.** Defining problems, identifying options and weighing trade offs.
5. **Implementation.** Why good policies fail, and what helps them succeed.
6. **Communication.** Writing and presenting for decision makers and the public.

## Assessment

Participants complete short weekly exercises and a final policy brief on a topic of their choice, which they present at the closing workshop. Certificates are awarded on successful completion.
