Budgets are the most important policy documents a government produces, and among the least read. This short course demystifies them.

## Weekly topics

1. The budget documents and where to find them
2. Revenue: where the money comes from
3. Expenditure: where it goes, and what is already committed
4. Deficits, debt and what sits outside the budget

Each week includes a short exercise using real budget documents.
