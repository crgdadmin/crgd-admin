CRGD offers colleges a ready made elective course in policy research, designed to fit within university regulations for value added or elective courses. Students learn research methods by applying them to a question in their own locality, under the guidance of college faculty and CRGD researchers.

Colleges interested in offering the elective are invited to write to us.
