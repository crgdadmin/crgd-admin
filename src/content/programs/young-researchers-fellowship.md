The Young Researchers Fellowship is designed for people who cannot take a full time fellowship but want serious research experience. Fellows work on a focused question set with a CRGD supervisor, meet the cohort once a month, and produce a short paper at the end.

Applications are reviewed as they arrive until the closing date.
