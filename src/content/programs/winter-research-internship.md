Interns join a research vertical and work alongside a CRGD researcher on a live project. Typical tasks include literature reviews, data collection and analysis, field visits, interviews and drafting short pieces.

Each intern completes an internship report, and the best are published in our [Internship Reports](/publications/internship-reports) series.
