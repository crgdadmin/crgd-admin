The CRGD Public Policy Fellowship is our flagship program for early career researchers. Each fellow joins one of CRGD's research verticals and designs, carries out and publishes a policy research project over nine months.

## What fellows do

- **Months 1 and 2.** Training in research design, methods and ethics. Refining the research question with supervisors.
- **Months 3 to 6.** Field work, data collection and analysis.
- **Months 7 to 9.** Writing, review, and presenting findings to people who can act on them.

Fellows also take part in CRGD's colloquia, mentor members of campus clubs and contribute to commentary and explainers.

## How to apply

Send the following to the careers address with "Public Policy Fellowship 2027" in the subject line:

1. A CV of no more than two pages
2. A statement of no more than 800 words on the research question you would like to pursue, why it matters and how you would approach it
3. One piece of writing you are proud of
4. The names and contact details of two referees

Shortlisted applicants will be invited to an interview in December.
