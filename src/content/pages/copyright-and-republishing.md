CRGD wants its work to be read as widely as possible. Unless a publication says otherwise, its text is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## You may

- republish our articles, briefs and reports in print or online, in full or in part
- translate them into other languages
- use our charts and tables in your own work
- quote from our publications

## On these conditions

1. **Credit the author and CRGD**, for example: *"This article was first published by CRGD: The Public Policy Bridge."*
2. **Link to the original** on our website where you can.
3. **Do not change the meaning.** You may edit for length and house style, but you must not alter the substance, and you must indicate if you have made significant changes.
4. **Do not imply endorsement.** You must not suggest that CRGD or the author endorses you, your organisation or your use of the work.

## Exceptions

- **Photographs and illustrations** are often licensed to us by others and are not covered by this licence unless stated. Please ask before reusing them.
- **Opinion pieces and expert views** by external contributors may carry different terms. Check the publication, or ask us.
- **Our name and logo** are not covered by this licence. See [media enquiries](/media-enquiries).

## Please tell us

You do not need permission to republish under these terms, but we would like to know. Write to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) with a link to your republication.
