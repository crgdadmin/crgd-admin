## About CRGD

**What is CRGD?**
The Centre for Research on Governance and Development (CRGD): The Public Policy Bridge is an independent, non-partisan research and advocacy institution dedicated to advancing liberty, knowledge, democracy, accountable governance, and development. It is headquartered in Thiruvananthapuram, Keralam. Read [about CRGD](/about).

**Is CRGD part of the government?**
No. CRGD is independent of government. It may carry out research commissioned by public bodies, but only on terms that protect its independence. See [independence and funding](/independence-and-funding).

**Is CRGD linked to a political party?**
No. CRGD is non partisan. It does not accept funding from political parties and does not support or oppose any party or candidate.

**Who funds CRGD?**
Individual supporters, foundations, research grants and, under strict conditions, commissioned work. Major sources are listed in our [annual reports](/annual-reports).

## Research and publications

**Can I read CRGD's publications for free?**
Yes. All our publications are free to read online and to download.

**Can I republish a CRGD article?**
Most of our publications can be republished free of charge with credit. See [copyright and republishing](/copyright-and-republishing).

**How do I suggest a research topic?**
Write to us through the [contact page](/contact). We read every suggestion, and several of our projects began this way.

**Can I write for CRGD?**
Yes. See [submissions](/submissions) for what we publish and how to pitch.

**I found an error in a publication. What should I do?**
Please tell us through the [contact page](/contact). We correct errors promptly and openly, as described in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy).

## Programs and learning

**Who can apply for fellowships and internships?**
Eligibility differs by program. Each [program page](/programs) lists who can apply, the duration, the stipend and how to apply.

**Do I need to be from Kerala?**
No. Many programs welcome applicants from across India. Some field work requires the ability to speak Malayalam, which will be stated in the call.

**How do I start a CRGD Policy Club at my college?**
See [campus clubs](/programs/campus-clubs). You will need a faculty supporter and a small group of interested students.

**Are CRGD Academy courses free?**
Some are free; others charge a modest fee to cover costs, with scholarships for those who need them. Fees are stated on each [course page](/learning/courses).

## Events

**Are CRGD events open to the public?**
Most are, and most are free. Some roundtables are invitation only so that participants can speak freely; we usually publish a summary afterwards.

**Are events recorded?**
Public lectures and panels are usually recorded and published on the event page and our video channel.
