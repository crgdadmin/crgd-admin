CRGD's researchers are available to journalists for background briefings, on the record comment and interviews in English and Malayalam, on subjects within their expertise.

## Contact the media team

Email [{{mediaEmail}}](mailto:{{mediaEmail}}) with your name, publication, deadline and the subject you are working on. We aim to respond the same working day, and sooner for urgent deadlines.

For broadcast interviews, please give as much notice as you can and tell us whether the interview is live or recorded.

## What we can offer

- **Comment** on policy developments within our research areas
- **Data and charts** from our publications, with explanations of what they show and do not show
- **Embargoed copies** of major reports ahead of release, for journalists who agree to respect the embargo
- **Introductions** to the people and communities involved in our field research, where they have agreed to speak to the media

## Using our work

You are welcome to quote our publications and reproduce our charts with credit to CRGD and a link to the original where possible. Our [copyright and republishing](/copyright-and-republishing) page explains the terms.

When describing CRGD, please use: *"The Centre for Research on Governance and Development (CRGD): The Public Policy Bridge, an independent, non-partisan research and advocacy institution based in Thiruvananthapuram."*

## Logo and photographs

Our logo and a selection of photographs of our people and events are available on request for editorial use. Please do not alter the logo or use it in a way that suggests CRGD endorses a product, party or campaign.

## Speakers' views

CRGD researchers speak in their personal capacity on the basis of their research. CRGD does not take institutional positions on behalf of any political party.
