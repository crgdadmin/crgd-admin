## Our standards

**Accuracy.** Facts are checked against primary sources wherever possible. Figures are sourced. Quotations are exact and attributed.

**Fairness.** We represent opposing views accurately and in their strongest form. People and institutions we criticise are given an opportunity to respond where appropriate.

**Clarity.** We write in plain language, explain technical terms and put conclusions first.

**Independence.** Our editorial decisions are made by CRGD alone, in line with our [independence and funding policy](/independence-and-funding).

**Non partisanship.** We do not endorse or oppose political parties or candidates. We assess policies on their merits, whoever proposes them.

## Institutional work and personal opinion

CRGD does not take institutional positions on most questions. Research publications such as policy briefs and reports present the findings and recommendations of their authors, reviewed under our [research standards](/research-standards).

Opinion pieces, expert views, blogs and young thoughts express the personal views of their authors. They are edited for accuracy and quality, but publication does not mean CRGD agrees with them. Each carries a note to that effect.

## Language

We publish mainly in English and, increasingly, in Malayalam. Translations are reviewed by a second speaker for accuracy. Where a translation differs from the original, the original language version prevails.

## Corrections

If we make a factual error, we correct it as quickly as possible and add a dated note to the corrected publication explaining what was changed. We do not silently change published work. Minor typographical corrections that do not alter meaning may be made without a note.

To request a correction, please [write to us](/contact) with the publication title, the error and, where possible, a source for the correct information. We acknowledge requests within five working days.

## Republishing

Most CRGD publications may be republished free of charge with credit. See [copyright and republishing](/copyright-and-republishing).

## Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to a correction request or complaint, you may ask for it to be reviewed by a member of the Governing Board who was not involved in the original decision.
