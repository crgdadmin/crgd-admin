CRGD's research is free to read, its events are mostly free to attend and its fellowships and internships pay a stipend. None of that is possible without support from people and institutions who believe that public policy should be made with better evidence and more open debate.

## What your support makes possible

- **A fellowship** for a young researcher to spend six months studying a public problem and publishing the results.
- **Field work** that takes our researchers to the communities most affected by a policy, so that their voices shape our recommendations.
- **Free public events** where citizens, officials and experts can reason together.
- **Open publications** that anyone can read, share and republish without paying.
- **Campus clubs** that bring policy debate to colleges across Kerala.

## Ways to give

**Individual contributions.** Contributions of any size are welcome, as a single gift or a regular monthly contribution. See the bank transfer and online options below.

**Sponsor a fellowship or a program.** Individuals, families and organisations can sponsor a named fellowship, a lecture series or a campus program. Sponsors are acknowledged, and have no role in the selection of fellows or the content of their work.

**Institutional partnerships.** Foundations, trusts and organisations can support research verticals or specific projects under our [independence and funding policy](/independence-and-funding).

**Give your time.** Professionals can volunteer as [mentors](/people/mentors), guest teachers in the CRGD Academy or reviewers of draft research.

**Give your expertise.** If you hold data, documents or experience that could help our research, we would like to hear from you.

## Our promise to supporters

- We will never let a contribution influence our findings.
- We will acknowledge your support, or keep it private if you prefer, subject to our disclosure rules for large contributions.
- We will report every year on how we used the money, in our [annual reports](/annual-reports).
