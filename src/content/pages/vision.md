<p class="lead">We imagine a Kerala, and an India, in which public decisions are made carefully, explained honestly and judged by what they achieve for people.</p>

## The society we work towards

**Where evidence comes first.** Every significant policy is informed by the best available evidence about the problem it addresses, the options available and the likely consequences. Where evidence is missing, government invests in finding it.

**Where disagreement is productive.** Citizens, experts and political leaders can disagree in good faith, on the basis of shared facts, in forums designed for reasoning rather than shouting.

**Where institutions are trusted because they are trustworthy.** Legislatures scrutinise, auditors are heeded, courts are timely, and public bodies explain their decisions. Trust is earned through transparency and accountability.

**Where no one is invisible.** Policy is designed with the participation of the people it affects, including those who have historically had the least voice: Adivasi and Dalit communities, fishing communities, persons with disabilities, migrant workers, women and the elderly.

**Where young people see public life as worth their talent.** The best minds of each generation choose to work on public problems, in government, in research, in civil society and in politics, and are equipped to do it well.

## Our part in it

No single institution can bring this about. CRGD's contribution is to be a reliable bridge: between research and those who decide, between government and the public, and between today's practitioners and tomorrow's. Our [mission](/mission) sets out how.
