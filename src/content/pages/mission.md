<p class="lead">Our mission is to bridge research, citizens and decision makers through independent, rigorous and accessible policy work.</p>

We pursue it in five ways.

## 1. Research that answers real questions

We study the questions that decision makers and citizens actually face, across [eighteen research verticals](/research). We use the methods each question requires: data analysis, field surveys, interviews, legal review, comparison and case study. We publish our methods and, wherever possible, our data.

## 2. Translation without distortion

We present findings in forms people can use: two page [policy briefs](/publications/policy-briefs) for decision makers, [explainers](/publications/explainers) for the public, [data analysis](/publications/analysis) for journalists and full [reports](/publications/policy-reports) for specialists. Simplifying never means misrepresenting.

## 3. Dialogue across divides

Through [dialogues](/learning/dialogue), [colloquia](/learning/colloquium), roundtables and public [events](/events), we bring together people who would not otherwise sit at the same table: officials and activists, scholars and practitioners, members of different parties, and the citizens most affected by a policy.

## 4. Building the next generation

Through [fellowships](/programs/fellowships), [internships](/programs/internships), the [Co-ordinator Program](/programs/coordinator-program), [campus clubs](/programs/campus-clubs) and the [CRGD Academy](/learning/academy), we give students and young professionals the skills and experience to work on public problems.

## 5. Following through

We stay with an issue after a report is published. We track whether recommendations are adopted, what happens when they are, and what we got wrong. Our [Impact](/impact) pages record the results honestly.

## Our values

- **Independence.** Our conclusions are our own. No funder, government or party can buy or veto them.
- **Rigour.** We check our work, invite challenge and correct our errors in public.
- **Openness.** We are transparent about our methods, our data and our funding.
- **Respect.** We treat the people we study and the people we disagree with as fellow citizens.
- **Usefulness.** We judge our work by whether it helps someone make a better decision.
