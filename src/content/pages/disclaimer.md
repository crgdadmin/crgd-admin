CRGD is an independent research institution. Its publications are intended to inform public debate and decision making.

**Views of authors.** Unless stated otherwise, publications present the views of their authors. Opinion pieces, expert views, blogs and young thoughts express personal views and do not represent an institutional position of CRGD.

**No professional advice.** Nothing on this website is legal, financial, medical or other professional advice. Readers should seek appropriate professional advice before acting on any information here.

**Accuracy.** We take care to ensure accuracy, but we cannot guarantee that all information is complete, current or free from error. Data from official and third party sources is reported as published by those sources. We correct errors as described in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy).

**No endorsement.** References to organisations, products, services or individuals do not imply endorsement. Mention of a political party, government or public figure in our work is for the purposes of analysis and does not imply support or opposition.

**External links.** CRGD is not responsible for the content of external websites linked from this website.

**Placeholder content.** While the website is being completed, some pages contain sample text and images marked "Placeholder". These are illustrations of layout and do not describe real people, publications or events.
