Our email updates are the easiest way to follow CRGD's work. Each edition includes:

- **New publications**, with a one line summary of each so you can decide what to read
- **Upcoming events**, with dates and registration links
- **Calls for applications** for fellowships, internships, courses and campus programs
- **One idea worth your time**: a short note from a CRGD researcher on something they have been reading or thinking about

We send updates about twice a month. We never sell or share your address, and every email includes a link to unsubscribe in one click. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) for details.

You can also follow CRGD on social media, or subscribe to our [RSS feed](/rss.xml) to receive every new publication in a feed reader.
