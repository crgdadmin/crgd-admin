## About the book

*The Public Policy Bridge: Essays on Governing Kerala* brings together researchers, practitioners and young scholars to examine the questions that will shape the state over the next twenty five years: public finances and federal relations, an ageing society, the changing nature of work and migration, climate risk, urbanisation, technology, and the health of democratic institutions.

Each essay is written for an informed general reader. Together they offer a picture of a society that has achieved much and faces new challenges that its past successes do not automatically equip it to meet.

## Contents

1. Introduction: Why Kerala needs a bridge between evidence and decisions
2. The fiscal question
3. Federalism from the state's point of view
4. Growing old in Kerala
5. Work, migration and return
6. The coast and the hills: living with climate risk
7. Neither town nor village: the urban future
8. Technology and the state
9. Institutions and the people they serve
10. Conclusion: An agenda for the next generation
