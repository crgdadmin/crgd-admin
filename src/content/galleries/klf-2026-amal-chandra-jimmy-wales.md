Our Founder and President, [Amal Chandra](/people/amal-chandra), with Jimmy Wales, internet entrepreneur and co-founder of Wikipedia, the non-profit free encyclopaedia and a remarkable experiment in shared knowledge, at the ninth Kerala Literature Festival at Kozhikode Beach in January 2026.

Their conversation ranged across emerging currents in cyberspace and innovation, reaffirming that the digital commons remains one of humanity's most hopeful inventions.
