[Ashraf Nehal](/people/ashraf-nehal), CRGD's Director of Global Affairs and Climate Change, was a speaker at **Powering Sustainable Urban Futures: The Nexus of Faith, Community Action, and Clean Energy Transitions**, a session at COP31, the UN Climate Change Conference in Antalya, Türkiye.

The session took place on Saturday 14 November 2026 at the International Youth Nuclear Congress (IYNC) Pavilion in the Antalya EXPO Center, with the Clean Energy Forum, the Tzu Chi Foundation and The Ramphal Institute. It asked how faith communities, local action and the shift to clean energy can together make cities more sustainable.

The panel:

- Itoro Victor-Akpan, The Ramphal Institute (moderator)
- Saikat Das, Clean Energy Forum
- Tiffany Tu, Tzu Chi Foundation
- William Brammwell, The Association of Commonwealth Universities
- Ashraf Nehal, University of Oxford

Ashraf's work spans the United Nations Environment Programme's Faith for Earth Coalition, youth engagement and interfaith dialogue on climate action, and he serves on the Global Steering Committee of the Children and Youth Major Group to UNEP. At CRGD he leads work on global affairs and climate change.
