Dr. Shashi Tharoor, Member of Parliament, released the logo of the Centre for Research on Governance and Development (CRGD). Sharing the photograph, he wrote:

> Pleased to release the logo of a new think-tank, the Centre for Research on Governance and Development, established by my young friend [Amal Chandra](/people/amal-chandra). Wishing him and his associates success, growth and impact in this promising new venture.

CRGD thanks Dr. Tharoor for his good wishes.
