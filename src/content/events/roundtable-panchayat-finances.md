This roundtable brings together presidents and members of panchayats and municipalities, officials of the local self government department, members of past State Finance Commissions and researchers to discuss the findings of CRGD's report [Panchayat Finances After Three Decades of Decentralisation](/publications/policy-reports/panchayat-finances-three-decades).

## Questions for discussion

1. How can local governments raise more of their own revenue fairly?
2. What would make the flow of funds from the state more predictable?
3. How can smaller panchayats get the technical support they need?
4. How can grama sabhas play a stronger role in reviewing spending?

A summary of the main points, without attribution, will be published afterwards.
