This project puts the recommendations of our brief on a [care guarantee for older citizens](/publications/policy-briefs/care-guarantee-older-citizens) into practice in a small number of partner local governments.

## Components

1. A consent based register of older residents who need support
2. Home visits by trained community caregivers
3. A day care centre in a public building
4. Training and certification for caregivers
5. An independent evaluation
