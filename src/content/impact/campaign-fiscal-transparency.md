## The goal

That the state publishes, alongside each budget, a single statement of all borrowing by state owned bodies that is serviced from the budget, all guarantees with an estimate of their likely cost, and a ten year repayment schedule.

## Why

As our brief [Bringing Off Budget Borrowing into the Light](/publications/policy-briefs/off-budget-borrowing-transparency) argues, transparency about the full picture of public obligations is a precondition for an informed debate about borrowing.

## What we are doing

- Briefing legislators of all parties and the relevant legislative committees
- Publishing an annual reading of the budget that assembles the available information
- Working with journalists to explain the issue to the public
