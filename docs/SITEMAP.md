# The brief, item by item

## Home page

| Brief | Delivered |
| --- | --- |

## Reference websites

The structure draws on the conventions of established think tanks (an about section with governance and annual reports, research by programme area, people profiles linked to their work, publication types with filters, events archives, a media centre and a clear support page). No text, design or code was copied from any of them; all writing on this site is original.
